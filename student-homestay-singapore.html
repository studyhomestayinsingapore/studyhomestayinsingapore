Guide for parents

Student homestay in Singapore:
what families should look for.

For a student moving to Singapore, accommodation is only one part of settling in. Families may also care about daily routines, meals, adult guidance, communication and whether the student has a calm place to study and recharge.

TT Family offers a family-style student homestay in Singapore for international and local students, combining accommodation with practical guardian care in a landed family home.

Choosing well

Five questions to ask before choosing a homestay

1

Who is present day to day?

Understand who the student can approach for practical help and how guardianship works in everyday life.

2

What routines are provided?

Ask about meals, study time, household expectations and the structure that helps a young person settle into school life.

3

How are parents kept informed?

Clear communication matters, particularly for families overseas. Agree how important updates and concerns will be shared.

4

Can we see the home?

A video or in-person tour helps families understand the bedroom, shared areas, study environment and household setting.

TT Family approach

More than a room

Our approach centres on a small-scale family environment: a trusted adult at home, nutritious meals, sensible routines, personal space and ongoing parent communication. Families can arrange an online introduction and home tour before deciding.

When enquiring, it helps to share the student's age, school, expected arrival date and intended length of stay so we can discuss fit and availability.
